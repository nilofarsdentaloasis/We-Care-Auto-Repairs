help Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Everything you need to know about our service standards, warranties, spare parts, and insurance.

Do you use 100% genuine original spare parts? expand_more

Yes, absolutely. We source 100% authentic original parts directly from authorized distributors for BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Audi, Porsche, Hyundai, Mahindra, Tata, Toyota, and Japanese brands, complete with official manufacturer warranties.

How much time does car servicing and repair take? expand_more

Routine periodic maintenance, synthetic oil changes, and brake service are usually completed on the same day within 3 to 5 hours. Major engine overhauls, custom performance tuning, or full body painting are planned with a clear timeline agreed beforehand.

Do you provide warranty on mechanical repairs and service? expand_more

Yes. All mechanical repairs, gearbox overhauls, and electrical work come with our workshop workmanship warranty, and all replacement spare parts carry their official manufacturer warranty.

How does the cashless accidental insurance claim process work? expand_more

We handle the entire process for you. Once your vehicle is brought to our Chikhali workshop, we coordinate with the insurance surveyor, complete the paperwork, get the claim approved, and repair your car with genuine parts.

Can I get photo and video updates on WhatsApp while my car is serviced? expand_more

Yes! Sohail Mulani and our technician team believe in 100% transparency. You will receive clear photos and videos on WhatsApp showing replaced parts and service progress so you have total peace of mind.

Do you provide car pickup and drop service in Pune and PCMC? expand_more

Yes, we offer safe doorstep pickup and drop service across Pune, Chikhali, Moshi, Nigdi, Bhosari, Talawade, Pimpri-Chinchwad, Hinjawadi, Wakad, Baner, and nearby areas.
