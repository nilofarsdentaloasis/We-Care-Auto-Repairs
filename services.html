help Frequently Asked Questions

Got Questions? We Have Answers.

Everything you need to know about our workshop standards, warranties, parts sourcing, and insurance.

Do you exclusively use genuine OEM parts? expand_more

Yes, absolutely. We source 100% authentic OEM replacement parts directly from authorized factory distributors for BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Audi, Porsche, Hyundai, Mahindra, Tata, and Japanese marques, complete with official manufacturer warranties.

What is your typical turnaround time for periodic maintenance and major repairs? expand_more

Routine periodic maintenance, synthetic oil flushes, and brake services are typically completed within same-day turnaround (3 to 5 hours). Major engine overhauls, custom stage dyno builds, and full body resprays are assigned dedicated bay slots with a clearly documented milestone schedule.

Do you provide warranty on mechanical repairs and performance tuning? expand_more

Yes. All mechanical repairs, transmission rebuilds, and electrical jobs come with our atelier workmanship warranty, while replacement parts carry their official manufacturer warranty. Our ECU tunes are backed by live dyno verification logs.

How does the cashless accidental insurance claim process work? expand_more

We handle the entire process from start to finish. Once your vehicle is brought to our Chikhali atelier, our dedicated insurance claims manager coordinates the surveyor inspection, documentation, claim approval, and repairs with zero upfront hassle.

Can I get photo and video progress updates while my car is being worked on? expand_more

Yes! Sohail Mulani and our technician team maintain an open-book policy. You will receive high-resolution photo and video logs on WhatsApp at every stage of teardown, part inspection, replacement, and testing.

Do you provide pickup and drop service across Pune and PCMC? expand_more

Yes, we offer safe flatbed transport and doorstep driver pickup/drop service across Pune, Chikhali, Moshi, Pimpri-Chinchwad, Hinjawadi, Wakad, Baner, and surrounding areas.
