Originally hailing from Kurduwadi in Solapur district, Sohail Mulani arrived in Pune a decade ago with an unyielding dedication to automotive excellence and mechanical precision.

Starting at the grassroots level as a helper in an authorized Hyundai showroom, Sohail mastered engine assemblies, powertrain tolerances, and electrical schematics. His diagnostic talent quickly earned him key technical roles across Mahindra showroom and Tata showroom workshops, mastering common-rail injection systems, heavy-duty diesel engines, and complex CAN-bus wiring.

Armed with 10+ years of cross-brand technical mastery, Sohail established We Care Auto Repair in Chikhali, Pune. Today, the workshop is trusted by prominent corporate leaders, sports car owners, and luxury motorists across Pune for its uncompromising dealer-grade precision, genuine OEM parts, and spotless track record.
