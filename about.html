Originally from Kurduwadi in Solapur district, Sohail Mulani came to Pune 10 years ago with a passion for cars, strong dedication, and a commitment to honest work.

Starting as a helper in an authorized Hyundai showroom, Sohail learned mechanical repairs, engine assembly, and electrical wiring from the ground up. With continuous learning, he earned senior technician positions across Mahindra showroom and Tata showroom workshops, mastering diesel engines, 4x4 drivetrains, and modern computer scanning tools.

With over 10 years of showroom experience, Sohail started We Care Auto Repair in Chikhali, Pune. Today, car owners, local business leaders, and premium car drivers trust him for reliable servicing, original spare parts, and honest advice.
