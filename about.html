From Gritty Showroom Floors to Pune's Trusted Luxury Car Atelier

Originally hailing from Kurduwadi in Solapur district, Sohail Mulani arrived in Pune over a decade ago driven by pure passion for mechanical engineering and automotive technology.

Starting humbly as a helper in a certified Hyundai showroom, Sohail absorbed every facet of engine mechanics and electrical systems. His exceptional knack for diagnostics led him to work extensively with Mahindra showroom and subsequently Tata showroom workshops, mastering powertrain tolerances, rugged utility engineering, and computerized diagnostic architectures.

Drawing on a decade of rigorous hands-on experience across multiple manufacturer platforms, Sohail established We Care Auto Repair. Today, he is widely trusted by leading business leaders, luxury car collectors, and motoring enthusiasts throughout Pune for his meticulous workmanship, clean track record, and dealer-grade solutions.
